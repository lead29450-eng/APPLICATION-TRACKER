About Application Tracker

Application Tracker is a simple tool designed to help job seekers organise their own job applications. It helps you record where you applied, what position you applied for, important dates and the progress of each application.

It is an organiser, not a recruitment agency. It does not submit applications, guarantee interviews or promise employment. You remain responsible for checking vacancy information and submitting applications to employers through their official channels.

A few useful job-search habits

A consistent routine can make it easier to manage multiple applications.

1. Check the requirements

Read the full vacancy notice and confirm that you meet the essential requirements before applying.

2. Note the closing date

Give yourself enough time to prepare documents and submit your application before the deadline.

3. Keep a record

Record the employer, post title, reference number and date you submitted the application.

4. Prepare for interviews

Review the job requirements and prepare examples that show your skills and experience.

5. Follow up appropriately

Use the employer's stated communication process. Be patient and keep a note of any follow-up date.

6. Protect your information

Only send personal documents through official, trusted application channels. Never share your password.

Privacy Policy

What information is used: When you create an account, your email address is used for account access. Information you enter about applications — such as employer, job title, vacancy references, dates, status, job description and notes — is stored to provide the tracker service.

Where information is stored: The application uses Supabase authentication and database services. Your information is transmitted to and stored by those services. Do not use the tracker on a shared device unless you log out afterwards.

Documents: Application Tracker does not provide CV, identity document, certificate or other file uploads. Keep these documents securely on your own device and submit them directly to employers through their official application channels.

Your choices: You can edit or delete individual application records inside your account. Deleting an application record does not necessarily delete your account. For account deletion or privacy questions, the site owner must provide a working support contact before public launch.

Security: Reasonable technical measures are used, but no internet service can promise absolute security. Use a unique password and do not share your login details.

Updates: This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

Before public launch, the site owner should add a real support contact and confirm the final data-retention and account-deletion process.
